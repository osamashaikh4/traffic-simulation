Smart Traffic Monitoring and Management

A web prototype that shows how a traffic signal could respond to demand at a junction in Bangladesh: a sample vehicle count is classified as Low, Medium or High, and a green-light duration is recommended using simple, transparent rules.

{{ icon('play', 16) }} Try the simulation

How it works

1GenerateA random whole number of vehicles from {{ min_count }} to {{ max_count }}.
2ClassifyThe count is matched to the Low, Medium or High density band.
3RecommendEach band has a green-time range; more vehicles in the band means more green time.
4Save & showThe result is stored in MySQL and shown on the dashboard, history and charts.

Included

  • Website with a simulation, dashboard, history and charts
  • Simulated vehicle counts (0–50) and manual test values
  • Rule-based Low / Medium / High classification
  • Recommended green time (editable rules)
  • MySQL storage of every run, with CSV export
  • Animated junction, including a fixed-timing comparison

Not included

  • Live cameras, road sensors, GPS or IoT devices
  • Control of real traffic lights
  • Machine learning or traffic prediction
  • Maps, route guidance or multiple junctions
  • Emergency-vehicle priority and user accounts
{{ icon('alert', 20) }} This is an academic demonstration. Vehicle counts are simulated, the thresholds and green times are illustrative values rather than traffic-engineering standards, and nothing here controls a real signal.
